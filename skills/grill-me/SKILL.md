---
name: grill-me
description: Stress-test a plan and turn it into execution notes.
---

Interview me on the plan until we have a usable execution brief.

For each branch, ask the smallest useful next question and give your recommended answer.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.

End with a short note that captures:
- the decision we made
- options ruled out
- open risks or unknowns
- which agent should own the next step
- a copy-paste task prompt for execution
