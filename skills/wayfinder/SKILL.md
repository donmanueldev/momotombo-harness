# Wayfinder

Use this when the destination is clear but the route is not.

## How it works

- Define the destination first.
- Create a map with explicit decision tickets (not implementation slices).
- Tag tickets as `wayfinder:research`, `wayfinder:prototype`, `wayfinder:grilling`, or `wayfinder:task`.
- Resolve one decision ticket at a time and keep the map updated.

## Rule

Do not turn wayfinder into implementation-by-default. The goal is navigation and decision clarity first.
